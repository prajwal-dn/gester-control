Sign language guide

The fingerspelling alphabet. Make each hand shape in front of the camera and hold it steady until the letter appears.

Space

Press Add space, or lower your hand for a moment after a letter. The next letter starts a new word.

Back

Backspace removes the last letter or space.

Clear

Clear removes the whole sentence so you can start again.

1

Hold each sign steady for a moment. The letter is added only when the shape stays the same.

2

Use good light and keep your whole hand inside the camera view.

3

Keep your hand about an arm's length from the camera, with the back of the hand clear of other objects.

4

J and Z need a small movement. Draw the letter in the air slowly.

Teach the app a sign

Choose Left or Right hand first. Type a letter or a word, hold the sign in front of the camera and press Record. Move your hand a little while it records. Record each sign 3 times, then press Train.
